The Magic of Tarot and Astrology, for buyers of the $9 course.

| Page | Live at | What it is |

| `index.html` | [/](https://sunnysink.github.io/tarot-and-astrology/) | **Course Companion.** Lesson hub, search across the whole course, 20 seasonal spreads, Reading Lab, notes and export |
| `dashboard/index.html` | [/dashboard/](https://sunnysink.github.io/tarot-and-astrology/dashboard/) | **Your Dashboard.** Every training and download in one place with tick-off boxes. Replaces the old Google Sheet |

## Both pages share one design system

Rose, teal, violet and gold on frosted glass over a pink and mint wash.
**System fonts on purpose**, so nothing external has to load.

**The tokens live in `brand/mota-design-system.md` in the private biz-brain repo.**
Change them there first, then copy into both pages. They are duplicated here by
necessity, since these are standalone files with no build step.

## The dashboard's links

23 items across 6 sections, taken from the original delivery sheet on 2026-08-07.
The full inventory is at `content/mota-delivery-inventory.md` in biz-brain.

⚠️ **Nine of them are Google Drive files.** A wrong sharing permission on any one
of them becomes a support thread, so spot-check them in a private window before
this page goes out to buyers.

Progress saves to the reader's own device with `localStorage`. Nothing is
collected, nothing is sent anywhere, and there is no account.
